Arquivos (nesta pasta):
- `block-pop-crazygames.zip`: o jogo (index.html + assets, com o SDK do CrazyGames). Gerar de novo: `./scripts/build-crazygames.sh`.
- `block-pop-index.html`: o mesmo jogo num arquivo só, para quando o antivírus bloqueia o .zip: salvar como `index.html` numa pasta e compactar no computador (botão direito → Compactar para arquivo ZIP).
- Capas: `cover-1920x1080.png` (paisagem), `cover-800x1200.png` (retrato), `cover-800x800.png` (quadrada).

**Name:** Block Pop

**Category:** Puzzle (tags: block, puzzle, 1010, casual, brain)

**Short description:**
Drag colorful blocks onto the 8x8 grid, fill rows and columns to clear them and chain combos for huge scores!

**Description:**
Block Pop is a relaxing but challenging block puzzle. You get 3 pieces at a time: drag them onto the grid and complete full rows or columns to make them pop. Clear several lines at once or keep clearing move after move to build combos and multiply your points. The board starts with scattered blocks and the pieces get bigger as your score grows, so plan ahead and always leave room for the next piece. Complete the daily mission, beat your best score and see how far you can go!

**Controls:**
- Mouse: click and drag a piece onto the grid.
- Touch: drag a piece with your finger (the piece is shown above your finger).

**Ads:** rewarded video on "Continue (watch video)" (clears the middle of the board once per game) and midgame ad on "Play again".
